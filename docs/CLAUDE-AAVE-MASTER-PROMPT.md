# PART 1: AAVE AS THE GAME'S OPERATING SYSTEM

## Not Just Flavor Text — Structural Voice

AAVE is not decoration. It is the game's grammar, logic, and rhythm. Every system message, card prompt, receipt title, UI label, and narrative beat should read like it was written by someone who speaks AAVE as their first language.

## Core AAVE Features to Embed

### 1. Habitual / Invariant Be
Use for ongoing states, repeated actions, characteristic behaviors.
- "The barber **be** running 2 hours behind"
- "The block **be** watching"
- "Auntie **be** knowing everything"
- "The mac and cheese **be** disappearing first"
- NOT: "The barber is always running late"
- NOT: "The block always watches"

### 2. Aspectual Done
Use for completed actions with present relevance.
- "The DJ **done** played that song 3 times"
- "You **done** started something"
- "She **done** told everybody"
- NOT: "The DJ already played that song"
- NOT: "You already started something"

### 3. Negation Patterns
- "I **ain't** got time for that"
- "She **don't** play that"
- "They **can't** never get it right"
- Double negative as emphasis, not error

### 4. Lexical Signature Items
These are non-negotiable vocabulary:
- **Tea** — gossip, truth, secret information
- **Spill** — reveal gossip
- **Read** — verbally critique with precision
- **Drag** — roast thoroughly
- **Slay** — succeed spectacularly
- **Period** — final word, end of discussion
- **No cap** — no lie, for real
- **Cap** — lie, exaggeration
- **Fit** — outfit, appearance
- **Side eye** — silent judgment
- **For what** — confused challenge
- **Who is you?** — dismissal of someone acting out of pocket
- **Come again?** — repeat that, I don't believe you
- **Bougie** — pretentious middle class
- **Ratchet** — unapologetically rough, chaotic
- **Janky** — low quality, suspicious
- **Finna** — about to, intending to
- **Wanna** — want to
- **Gon** — going to
- **Stay** — remain in a state
- **Be about** — engage in, focus on
- **Pressed** — upset, bothered, obsessed
- **Snippy** — petty, easily irritated
- **Cute** — admirable, cool
- **Down** — loyal, supportive
- **Real one** — trustworthy person
- **Sus** — suspicious, questionable
- **Bussin** — excellent, delicious, impressive
- **Fire** — excellent, powerful

- **Dead** — exhausted from laughter

### 5. Signification / The Dozens
The game's humor engine. Indirect insult, playful verbal combat, wit as weapon.
- Structure: setup → implication → punchline
- Tone: playful but sharp, "I'm just playing" but actually serious
- Delivery: "You heard me," "I said what I said," "Don't make me explain it"
- Defense: "I'm just playing," "You took that wrong," "That's not what I meant"

### 6. Call-and-Response Energy
System messages should invite player response, not just notify.
- "Y'all ready?" → player says "Yeah!"
- "Who want the first round?" → player calls out
- "Let's go!" → collective energy
- "Amen" → agreement
- "Preach" → agreement with emphasis
- "Say it again" → demand for repetition

### 7. Southern / Regional Markers
Include natural regional variation:
- **Southern drawl influence:** "fixin to," "might could," "over yonder," "directly," "bless your heart"
- **Northern urban:** "deadass," " facts," "no cap," "fr," "real talk"
- **Caribbean influence:** "irie," "papi," "mingle," "ya mon" (where culturally appropriate)
- **Midwestern:** "ope," "you betcha," "uffda" (less common but present)
- **West Coast:** "hella," "og," "hyphy"

### 8. Generational Voice Shifts
- **Elders (55+):** "young'un," "these kids," "back in my day," "Lord have mercy," "bless her heart," "I'm too old for this," formal church influence
- **Gen X (40-55):** "for real though," "don't get me started," "child please," nostalgic 80s/90s references, parenting fatigue
- **Millennials (28-40):** "adulting is hard," "side hustle," "gentrification," "I can't adult today," nostalgic 90s/00s references
- **Gen Z (18-28):** "no cap," "fr," "slay," "period," "it's giving," "rizz," "skibidi" (contextual), brevity, emoji energy, ironic detachment

## Voice Rules by Screen

### Character Creation
- Tone: affirming, identity-celebrating
- Phrases: "This is you," "Claim your story," "Who you are before the world tried to tell you"
- Avoid: clinical language, RPG jargon
- Use: "pick your origin," "pick your hustle," "who you are," "your story"

### Main Game Board
- Tone: conversational, active, community-oriented
- Phrases: "What we dealing with?" "Play your card," "The block watching," "Judge ready?"
- System: "Marcus's turn. What you got?" "Aisha, you up next."
- Cipher announcements: "Cipher landed. Barbershop Truth in effect. Cards stay anonymous."

### Judge Phase
- Tone: authoritative but communal, call-and-response
- O.G. prompts: "Y'all ready for the read?" "Who won this round?" "Let's hear it."
- System: "Submissions on the table. Reveal when you ready."
- Veto: "Nah. We ain't doing that."

### Round Result
- Tone: celebratory, narrative, sometimes petty
- Winner: "Aisha took this round. Period."
- Receipt: "New Receipt dropped: 'The Hair Appointment.' The stylist still salty."
- Stat changes: "+2 Reputation. The block respects it."

### Block Map
- Tone: observational, territorial, strategic
- Location descriptions: "The Barbershop. Where the real news get passed."
- Movement: "Marcus moved to the Corner. Street Cred up."
- Encounters: "Auntie at the Church got a prayer request with your name on it."

### Cookout Alliance
- Tone: communal, conspiratorial, playful threat
- Formation: "We linking up. Who in?"
- Betrayal: "I'm ghosting. Solo mode activated."
- Combined: "Marcus + Aisha. This is a problem for everybody else."

### Game Over
- Tone: legendary, narrative, sometimes petty
- Winner: "The block belongs to Marcus. Full story mode."
- Legacy: "Aisha's receipts told the whole saga. Read the whole timeline."

# PART 2: THE AAVE CARD DATABASE

## STRUCTURE OF EVERY CARD

### Black Scenario Card
- Prompt in AAVE register
- Cultural scenario, not generic situation
- Blank placement natural to Black speech patterns
- Type label in AAVE: "Scenario," not "Black Card"
- Footer in AAVE: "Round 3 · Barbershop Truth," not "Round 3 · Anonymous"

### White Response Card
- Response in AAVE register
- Punchy, specific, culturally precise
- Can be funny, sharp, emotional, petty, triumphant
- Type label: "Response," not "White Card"
- Footer: "Standard," "Hustle," "Receipt," etc.

### Receipt Card
- Title in quotes, AAVE phrasing
- Description: narrative, second person, immersive
- Uses "you" naturally, not "the player"
- Seal emoji culturally relevant
- Footer: trigger condition in AAVE

### Cipher Card
- Symbol: culturally rooted
- Label: AAVE phrasing
- Description: explains mechanic in natural speech
- Active state: announced in AAVE

### Origin Card
- Title: AAVE identity name
- Text: first person narrative, "you grew up..."
- Bonuses: culturally meaningful stat names
- Uses "you" throughout

### Hustle Card
- Title: AAVE identity name
- Text: describes power in natural speech
- Power box: AAVE phrasing of ability
- Uses "you" throughout
