# Endings & Epilogue
## Canonical and secret endings with precise gating, real-world aftermath, and cultural resonance

### Endings
- Justice: legal route, evidence collected, betrayer exposed; block reforms around player's choices
- Power: street/violent route, high heat/reputation, one trust = 5; player becomes unspoken authority
- Ghost: secret route, origin secret used, low heat/reputation; player vanishes with cash
- Receipt King: all 13 receipts, all secrets, balanced trusts; player breaks block curse
- Death: heat = 10 in Act 3; player is killed by Marquez or police depending on path

### Real-World Inspiration
- Justice ending: inspired by real community reform movements, church-led activism, and press exposés
- Power ending: inspired by real block bosses, informal economies, and community control struggles
- Ghost ending: inspired by real witness protection, informal exits, and people who just disappear
- Receipt King ending: inspired by real archival research, truth commissions, and breaking cycles of trauma

### Humor in Endings
- Death ending: the block still jokes about you at the cookout; "he should've listened to Ray"
- Power ending: the block votes you in but immediately starts asking for favors
- Ghost ending: someone finds one of your receipts and posts it; the block goes crazy theorizing
- Justice ending: the church holds a celebration but the potluck is burnt; Jada saves it with her potato salad

### Chaos in Endings
- Multiple NPCs show up to the final confrontation uninvited
- The plan goes wrong in an unexpected way; you have to adapt
- Secret ending requires collecting all receipts; the final reveal is absurd but earned
- Epilogue shows that the block keeps evolving even after your story ends

### Mature Content in Endings
- Violence is implied not gratuitous; consequences are shown, not glorified
- Romance and betrayal are resolved or left open based on trust graph
- Money and power corrupt even well-intentioned characters
- Family secrets are revealed; some bonds break, some strengthen
- Language reflects the character's personality and situation

### Epilogue Rules
- 4-6 epilogue cards showing NPC fates based on player choices
- Each NPC outcome depends on final trust/reputation/heat
- Final stats screen with all secrets, flags, receipts collected
- New Game+ keeps unlocked secrets, resets story

### Real-World Aftermath Resonance
- No ending is perfectly happy; the block always costs something
- No ending is perfectly tragic; the block always keeps a piece of you
- The world keeps changing after your story ends
- Your receipts become someone else's history
