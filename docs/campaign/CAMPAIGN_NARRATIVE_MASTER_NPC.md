# NPC Relationship Web
## Reactive memory, dialogue assembly, betrayal telegraphing

### Core NPCs
- Ray: barbershop owner, former boxer, protective; speaks in 1990s barbershop wisdom
- Jada: bar manager, neighborhood information hub; 2000s social media savvy, present-day influencer adjacent
- Marquez: precinct contact, favors are debts; 1980s rookie cop energy, 1990s corruption arc
- Mr. Chen: bodega owner, shipment secret; Puerto Rican legacy, bilingual code-switching
- The Kid: young witness, family loyalty; 2000s kid, present-day teen; speaks in current slang
- Jenkins: retired, knows everyone’s secrets; 1970s-80s old head, Vietnam vet energy, jazz collector

### Relationship State
- allied: +2 trust modifier when both present
- neutral: no modifier
- rival: badmouths, locks joint scenes
- hostile: betrayal likely under pressure

### Dialogue Assembly
- Core line: always present
- Relationship line: if trust >= threshold
- State line: if world flag present
- Reactive line: last 3 player choices involving NPC
- Filler line: ambient optional lines

### Memory Importance
- 1-20: minor
- 21-50: notable
- 51-80: important
- 81-100: canon

### Betrayal Telegraphing
- Stopped sharing items
- Chose cards that hurt player
- Increased suspicion
- Avoided cooperation
- If visible, then betrayal is earned, not scripted

### Decade-Specific Dialogue Anchors
- 1980s NPC references: VHS, boomboxes, Kangols, payphones, arcade games, Run-DMC, Eddie Murphy
- 1990s NPC references: beepers, Blockbuster, Timberlands, doo-rags, Fresh Prince, Mary J. Blige
- 2000s NPC references: Sidekicks, iPods, MySpace, camera phones, Soulja Boy, crunk
- Present-day NPC references: TikTok, Instagram, Cash App, Venmo, streaming, drill, NFTs

### Humor Anchors by NPC
- Ray: dry, old-man humor; roasts you for your haircut or your outfit
- Jada: sharp, quick-witted; reads people in public; shade is her love language
- Marquez: dark, gallows humor; laughs at danger; suspicious of everything
- Chen: awkward dad humor; tries too hard; gives you expired snacks
- The Kid: chaotic child energy; says the wildest thing at the worst moment
- Jenkins: rambling, nostalgic; tangents that somehow become profound
