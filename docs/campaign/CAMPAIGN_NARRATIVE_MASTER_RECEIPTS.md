# Receipt Lore System
## 13 receipts with layered meanings, real-world objects, and discovery rules

### Mechanics
- Hidden discovery; no quest marker until found
- Inspection reveals lore fragment and possible flag
- Some receipts are mutually exclusive by path choice
- All 13 unlocks Secret Ending D: The Receipt King
- Persist across New Game+ as lore fragments only

### Real-World Object Anchors
- Receipts are physical objects that exist in real Black/urban life
- Each has surface utility, hidden meaning, and meta significance
- Objects chosen from actual neighborhood artifacts: Polaroids, payphone cards, VHS tapes, bodega price lists, court documents, handwritten notes

### Decade-Specific Receipts
- 1980s receipts: Polaroid photos, handwritten ledger pages, cassette tape labels, payphone card stubs
- 1990s receipts: beeper messages, Blockbuster receipts, VHS tape labels, handwritten rap lyrics
- 2000s receipts: Sidekick text printouts, MySpace comments, camera phone prints, burned CD labels
- Present-day receipts: Cash App screenshots, Venmo requests, Instagram DMs, TikTok comments, group chat screenshots
