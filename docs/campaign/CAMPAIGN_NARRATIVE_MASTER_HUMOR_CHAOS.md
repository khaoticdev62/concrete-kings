# Humor, Chaos & Funny Moments
## Pop culture, art, relationships, mature content across Black cultural decades

### Humor Rules
- Humor is survival: every punchline has a cost or a consequence
- Chaos is not random: it has roots in personality, relationships, and world state
- Pop culture references are time-period aware based on NPC age and player origin
- Mature content is authentic, not exploitative: relationships, money, violence, sex, and humor coexist

### Decade Cultural Anchors
- 1980s: crack epidemic, early hip hop, breakdancing, video arcades, boomboxes, gold chains, Kangol hats, fat laces, VHS, cable TV, Eddie Murphy, Run-DMC, Beastie Boys, NES, arcade culture, street corner ciphers
- 1990s: golden age hip hop, R&B boy bands, Tupac/Biggie, fresh prince, Martin, Living Single, Wu-Tang, A Tribe Called Quest, baggy jeans, Timberlands, doo-rags, cell phones becoming common, Blockbuster, arcade fighters, Sega Genesis, SNK, hair relaxer culture, cookout culture
- 2000s: 50 Cent, Jay-Z, Beyoncé, crunk, snap music, MySpace, iPods, Xbox, PlayStation 2, flat twists, sew-ins, reality TV, Soulja Boy, ringtones, camera phones, flash mobs, dance challenges
- Present day: streaming, TikTok, Instagram, Afrobeats, drill, sneaker culture, influencer economy, OnlyFans, podcast culture, electric scooters, food trucks, Black Lives Matter, cancel culture, meme stocks, NFTs, AI-generated art

### Humor Templates
- The Setup: NPC promises something impossible
- The Switch: player action turns promise into chaos
- The Punchline: world reacts; consequences land later

### Chaos Triggers
- High chaos NPC chooses unexpected card
- Party disagreement escalates publicly
- Secret objective collides with party goal
- World state shifts during scene
- Random event with comedic timing

### Funny Moment Types
- Social: roast battle, signifying contest, call-and-response gone wrong
- Physical: prop comedy, costume failure, dance battle interruption
- Situational: wrong place wrong time, mistaken identity, coincidence cascade
- Pop culture: reference mismatch, nostalgia bait, era collision

### Mature Content Guidelines
- Relationships: romance, jealousy, betrayal, loyalty, family tension
- Money: cash flow, debt, hustles, scams, get-rich-quick schemes
- Violence: threats, intimidation, fights, weapons, police encounters
- Sex: attraction, intimacy, reputation, rumors, adult choices
- Language: profanity used naturally, not for shock value

### Relationship Dynamics
- Romantic: exes, new attractions, secret affairs, public disputes
- Familial: parent-child tension, sibling rivalry, generational conflict
- Platonic: ride-or-die, fair-weather, frenemies, mentor-student
- Community: block politics, church politics, business partnerships

### Real-World Cultural Anchors
- Barbershops: community sanctuaries, economic engines, places of discourse
- Bodegas: Puerto Rican/Dominican legacy, community centers, credit systems
- Block culture: extended kin networks, informal economies, collective child-rearing
- Church culture: moral authority, social services, political organizing
- Street culture: ciphers, battles, codes of respect

### Comedy Legacy
- 1980s: Richard Pryor's raw storytelling, Eddie Murphy's crowd work
- 1990s: Martin's sitcom timing, Chris Rock's social commentary, Bernie Mac's gruff warmth
- 2000s: Dave Chappelle's sketch revolution, Kevin Hart's high-energy storytelling
- Present day: social media comedy, TikTok roast culture, podcast banter

### Art & Expression
- Graffiti evolution: tags → pieces → murals → gallery acceptance
- Hip hop as culture: DJing, MCing, breaking, graffiti, knowledge
- Fashion as identity: from Kangols to designer gear to streetwear hype
- Food as culture: soul food traditions, Caribbean fusion, vegan/health consciousness
