# Side Quest System
## Template-driven procedural quests with cultural texture and real-world inspiration

### Real-World Inspiration
- Barbershop contests: real annual events with cash prizes and neighborhood bragging rights
- Bodega quizzes: real proprietors who know every customer's order and business
- Block politics: real block associations, street presidents, and neighborhood beefs
- Underground economies: real side hustles, package runs, and informal credit systems

### Templates
- Missing: find what disappeared
- Escort: move someone through danger
- Investigate: gather 3 clues
- Intercept: stop a shipment or message
- Protect: keep a person or place safe
- Steal: take something specific
- Negotiate: settle without full payment

### Decade-Specific Side Quest Flavor
- 1980s: arcade challenges, boombox battles, breakdancing contests, payphone message runs
- 1990s: beeper code decoding, Blockbuster returns, basketball tournaments, house party setups
- 2000s: Sidekick text trails, MySpace friend recovery, ringtone challenges, camera phone photo hunts
- Present-day: TikTok challenges, Cash App heists, Instagram story hunts, drone deliveries

### Humor & Chaos Templates
- The Setup: NPC promises something simple
- The Switch: side quest escalates unexpectedly
- The Punchline: reward is ridiculous but useful
- Chaos: side quest interferes with main quest timing

### Mature Content
- Relationships: exes, secret affairs, family secrets exposed
- Money: debts, hustles, scams, get-rich-quick schemes
- Violence: threats, fights, weapons, police encounters
- Sex: attraction, intimacy, reputation, rumors
- Language: profanity used naturally, not for shock value

### Examples with Real-World Anchors
- SQ1_TheCat: cat is hiding from a dog that belongs to a drug dealer; rescuing it reveals a shipment schedule
- SQ2_RaysDebt: Ray owes money to a former client who now runs a different shop; collecting it involves a basketball game
- SQ3_ChenShipment: Chen's shipment is intercepted by a rival bodega; solving it requires a neighborhood-wide negotiation
- SQ4_JadasSecret: Jada's ex-husband has her journal; retrieving it involves a house party infiltration
- SQ5_OldMansStory: Jenkins' service medal was pawned; retrieving it involves a pawn shop raid
- SQ6_MarquezsFavor: Marquez wants you to plant evidence; the target is innocent but connected to the pipeline
- SQ7_BodegaQuiz: Chen tests your neighborhood knowledge; questions involve real 125th Street history
- SQ8_BarberContest: Ray enters a barbershowdown; you have to help him cheat without getting caught
- SQ9_PhoneHack: A burner phone contains the betrayer's identity; hacking it involves a mini-game
- SQ10_KingOfTheBlock: Win a 3-round social contest; the reward is political power on the block
