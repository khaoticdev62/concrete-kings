# PART 1: EXPANDED AAVE VOICE SYSTEM

## Complete Grammatical Framework

### Habitual Be (invariant be)
Use for ongoing states, repeated actions, characteristic behaviors, general truths.
- "The barber **be** running 2 hours behind"
- "The block **be** watching everything"
- "Auntie **be** knowing everybody's business"
- "The mac and cheese **be** disappearing first"
- "The DJ **be** playing the same 5 songs"
- "The church **be** having all the tea"
- "The corner store **be** raising prices every week"
- "The beauty shop **be** running 3 hours behind on Sundays"
- "Grandma **be** feeding you like you starving"
- "The HOA **be** making up rules"

### Aspectual Done
Use for completed actions with present relevance, recent past with ongoing effects.
- "The DJ **done** played that song 3 times already"
- "You **done** started something you can't finish"
- "She **done** told everybody your business"
- "They **done** messed around and fell out"
- "He **done** learned his lesson the hard way"
- "The landlord **done** raised rent 3 times this year"
- "Auntie **done** made her plate before everybody else"
- "The police **done** pulled everybody on the block over"
- "You **done** missed the whole cookout"
- "The bodega **done** run out of everything again"

### Negation Patterns
- "I **ain't** got time for that"
- "She **don't** play that"
- "They **can't** never get it right"
- "I **didn't** do nothing"
- "You **ain't** heard nothing yet"
- "We **don't** do that around here"
- "He **don't** know nothing about that"
- "They **ain't** gonna let you in without a ticket"
- "She **can't** nobody tell her nothing"
- "I **ain't** never seen that before"

### Perfect Aspect (done + been)
- "He **done been** working there for 10 years"
- "She **done been** through more than you know"
- "They **done been** trying to fix that for years"
- "I **done been** telling you that"
