# Concrete Kings - Narrative RPG Design Document
## Black Culture Card Game with CAH Vibes, RPG Depth

---

## 1. Core Concept Shift

**From:** Pick a funny white card to complete a black card prompt.  
**To:** Each player is a character on The Block. You play white cards to complete black-card scenarios, but your choices build your character's story, reputation, and relationships across rounds.

**Tagline:** *"Every mile costs. Every choice leaves receipts."*  
**Cultural Anchor:** Griot oral tradition, barbershop storytelling, block politics, cookout diplomacy, street entrepreneurship, community accountability.

---

## 2. The Block - Character Creation

Every player starts as a character on **The Block**. Before the first round, each player picks:

### Origin Stories (White Cards)
- "The kid who sold candy out of a locker"
- "The cousin who brought the whole family to the cookout"
- "The barber who heard every secret on the chair"
- "The DJ who kept the party going till 3am"
- "The auntie who runs the neighborhood bank"
- "The uncle who fixes everything with duct tape"
- "The student who organized the walkout"
- "The grandma who feeds the whole block"

### Character Stats (Tracked on Scoreboard)
Each character has 4 stats that evolve:

1. **Street Cred** - How much respect you have on The Block. High cred lets you challenge judges, low cred means people ignore you.
2. **Community** - How connected you are to neighbors. High community gives you allies, low means you're out here alone.
3. **Wisdom** - What you've learned from experience. High wisdom lets you see through schemes, low means you fall for every trick.
4. **Reputation** - How The Block remembers you. High reputation makes your wins stick, low means your wins get questioned.

**Starting values:** Each origin card has hidden stat bonuses that only the player sees.

---

## 3. The O.G. - Judge Role Evolution

Instead of a neutral judge, the judge becomes **The O.G.** - the elder who's seen it all.

### O.G. Powers:
- Can veto one submission per round if it feels "off-brand" for The Block
- Can add a **"Receipt"** - a consequence that haunts a player next round
- Can call a **"Cookout Summit"** - everyone negotiates a compromise instead of picking one winner
- Rotates every round, but O.G. with high Reputation gets to serve 2x more often

### O.G. Bonuses:
If you win as O.G., you gain +1 Reputation. If you lose as O.G., people question your judgment: -1 Street Cred.

---

## 4. The Cipher - Wildcard Dice Evolution

Dice rolls are now called **"The Cipher"** - unpredictable cultural twists that change the whole scenario.

### New Dice Effects:
1. **"Block Watch Called"** - The whole scenario shifts. Everyone re-plays from their hand. Chaos.
2. **"Receipts Dropped"** - A past decision comes back to haunt someone. The O.G. picks who.
3. **"Block Party Mode"** - Everyone wins this round. The narrative continues collectively.
4. **"Barbershop Truth"** - Cards are read aloud anonymously. No one knows who played what until after judging.
5. **"Side Door Effect"** - The lowest-Rep player gets one free submission from the discard pile.
6. **"Cipher Misfire"** - The black card gets discarded. New black card drawn. Free round.

### Dice Visual:
The dice shows a **spinner/dreidel-style cipher wheel** with cultural symbols instead of numbers: ☮ (peace), 🔥 (fire), 💰 (bread), 📻 (radio), 🎤 (mic), 👑 (crown).

---

## 5. Narrative Continuity - Story Threads

This is the **unique mechanic** that makes it a narrative RPG, not just a party game.

### The Receipt System
When a player wins a round, they earn a **"Receipt"** - a story thread that carries into future rounds.

**Example Receipts:**
- "You helped your cousin bail out of jail. Now they owe you."
- "You told that joke at the cookout. Auntie still side-eyeing you."
- "You invested in that side hustle. It's about to blow up or flop."
- "You stood up to the landlord. They're watching you now."
- "You brought the best dish. Everyone expects excellence forever."

### Receipt Resolution
Future black cards will reference active receipts:
- "Your cousin who owes you shows up at the cookout with ____"
- "Auntie who's still side-eyeing you says ____"
- "The landlord who's watching you just posted ____"

If a player resolves their receipt well (wins the round where it triggers), they get +2 Reputation and the thread closes. If they fail, they take -1 to all stats.

### Stacking Receipts
Players can have 3-5 active receipts at once. This creates dramatic tension: "I'm trying to run a legitimate business while my cousin is still out here wilding, my auntie is judging my life choices, AND the landlord just raised rent."

## 6. The Block Map - Spatial Storytelling

Instead of just a scoreboard, there's a visual **Block Map** that shows:

- Where everyone lives (houses, apartments, corners, the barbershop, the bodega, the park)
- Who's beefing (red lines)
- Who's linked up (green lines)
- Who's in debt (yellow markers)
- Where the cookout is happening (rotates)

**Gameplay Effect:** Location matters.
- If you're at the **barbershop**, you get +1 Wisdom for social maneuvers
- If you're at the **bodega**, you get +1 Street Cred for entrepreneurial plays
- If you're at the **park**, you get +1 Community for coalition plays
- If you're at **home**, you get +1 Reputation for personal wins

The O.G. can move one player per round: "You're sleeping on my couch tonight" or "Get out of my barbershop."

---

## 7. The Cookout - Alliance System

Players can form temporary alliances called **"The Cookout"** - everyone brings something, everyone shares the win.

### How it works:
- 2+ players can declare a Cookout before submitting
- They play their cards in sequence, creating a combined narrative
- The O.G. judges the collective story, not individual cards
- If the Cookout wins, ALL members get +1 Community and share the win
- If the Cookout loses, ALL members take -1 Reputation (they looked corny together)

### Cookout Bonuses:
- **3-person Cookout:** Can trigger a "Block Party" dice effect automatically
- **4+ person Cookout:** Can rewrite one black card prompt before judging
- **All-player Cookout:** The O.G. must pick at least 2 winners

### Betrayal Mechanic:
Anyone in a Cookout can **"Ghost the Cookout"** - secretly play for themselves instead of the alliance. If they win, they get +2 Reputation (clever) but all former allies get -2 Community (they got played).

---

## 8. The Hustle - Economic System

Players have a **Hustle** - their primary grind. This is chosen at character creation:

### Hustle Types:
1. **Street Hustler** - Good at quick wins, high risk/reward
2. **Community Organizer** - Builds alliances, low individual wins but high Community gains
3. **Creative** - Barbers, DJs, artists. Can "remix" black cards for better effect
4. **Entrepreneur** - Builds long-term advantages, slow burn but powerful
5. **Student** - Gains Wisdom fast, can study opponents' patterns
6. **Elder** - Starts with high Reputation, but loses Street Cred over time

### Hustle Mechanics:
- **Street Hustler:** Can re-roll the Cipher once per round
- **Organizer:** Can form Cookouts with +1 member bonus
- **Creative:** Can add one extra blank to any black card they play
- **Entrepreneur:** Keeps half their winnings even when they lose
- **Student:** Can peek at one opponent's hand per round
- **Elder:** Can veto the O.G.'s Receipt once per game

---

## 9. The Check-Ins - Round Structure

Each round follows this narrative flow:

1. **The Roll Call** - O.G. draws the black card. The Block Map updates. Active receipts are read aloud.
2. **The Cipher** - If the black card has dice, O.G. clicks it. The cipher wheel spins. Effect applies.
3. **The Move** - Players select their white cards in secret.
4. **The Reveal** - All submissions shown. O.G. picks winner (or Cookout wins).
5. **The Receipt** - Winner gets a new story thread. Stats update. The Block Map shifts.
6. **The Check-In** - Quick narrative summary: "What just happened on The Block this round?"

## 10. Real-World Scenarios - Card Design Philosophy

### Black Cards (Scenarios) are grounded in:
- **The Cookout:** Family dynamics, food politics, who brought what, who's dating who
- **Block Politics:** Landlord disputes, neighborhood watch, gentrification, city council shenanigans
- **The Grind:** Side hustles, job interviews, college apps, resume building
- **The Culture:** Music, fashion, slang, viral moments, Black Twitter
- **The History:** Civil rights, Black Wall Street, Juneteenth, reparations conversations
- **The Church:** Sunday service, potlucks, choir drama, deacon board politics
- **The HBCU Experience:** Homecoming, step shows, Greek life, protest culture
- **The Beauty Shop:** Hair politics, salon gossip, edge control debates, protective styles
- **The Barbershop:** Fade critiques, sports takes, relationship advice, neighborhood news

### White Cards (Responses) include:
- Specific cultural references, not generic humor
- Real scenarios that resonate: "the Airbnb that ruined the block party"
- Authentic dialogue: "Girl, put your degree on your résumé"
- Cultural touchstones: "a step show that started a beef"
- Economic realities: "a rent-controlled apartment with a view of the new luxury building"
- Family dynamics: "the cousin who posts every family business on Facebook"

## 11. Example Round Flow

**Black Card:** "The new luxury building just opened across the street and ____"

**Cipher Dice:** 🔥 (Barbershop Truth - anonymous play)

**Submissions:**
- Player 1 (Street Hustler): "a pop-up shop selling $10 lemonade"
- Player 2 (Organizer): "a community land trust proposal"
- Player 3 (Creative): "a mural that got painted over by 6am"
- Player 4 (Elder): "a protest that got co-opted by a corporation"

**O.G. Judgement:** Player 2 wins with the community land trust proposal.

**Receipt Earned:** "You organized the block against gentrification. The developer is now your nemesis. Watch for their moves."

**Stat Changes:**
- Player 2: +2 Community, +1 Reputation
- Player 4: +1 Wisdom (they called it)
- Player 1: -1 Street Cred (got out-hustled)

**Block Map Update:** Developer building now shows as "hostile" on the map.

## 12. Unique Selling Points

1. **Cultural Authenticity:** Not "diversity" content - this is written from inside the culture, using real scenarios, real dialogue, real stakes.
2. **Narrative Memory:** The Receipt system means your choices echo across rounds. This isn't just a party game; it's a story you build together.
3. **The Block Map:** Visual storytelling that makes the world feel alive.
4. **The O.G. System:** Rotating judge with real power creates dynamic politics, not just a passive arbiter.
5. **The Cipher:** Cultural twist mechanic that embraces chaos and unpredictability.
6. **Cookout Alliances:** Temporary cooperation with betrayal mechanics creates social dynamics beyond simple competition.
7. **Hustle Archetypes:** Each playstyle has unique powers, encouraging diverse strategies and replayability.

## 14. Content Guidelines

All content must pass the **"Block Test"**:
- Would this scenario actually happen on a real block?
- Does this reference resonate with Black cultural experience?
- Is this respectful of the culture, or exploiting it?
- Does this create meaningful narrative stakes, not just cheap laughs?
- Would an elder on The Block approve this?

**No stereotypes.** No performative "urban" content. No punching down within the community. The humor comes from shared experience, not caricature.

---

## 15. Name Evolution

**Current:** Concrete Kings  
**Full Title:** Concrete Kings: The Block Chronicles  
**Tagline:** "Every mile costs. Every choice leaves receipts."

The name works because:
- "Concrete" = the streets, the block, the foundation
- "Kings" = royalty, excellence, Black excellence
- "The Block Chronicles" = narrative, ongoing story, Griot tradition
