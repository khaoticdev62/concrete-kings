Full Detail / Aligned to 1280x720

NATIVE RESOLUTION: 1280x720
MAP CANVAS SIZE: 960x520
WORLD SIZE: 2400x1300 (Walkable district grid)

TOP-DOWN DISTRICT EXPLORATION
- Top-down / 3/4 hybrid walkable perspective.
- Viewport of 960x520 pixels scales crisply to the 1280x720 frame using nearest-filter scaling.
- Camera centers on the player avatar and clamps smoothly to the 2400x1300 world bounds.

DISTRICT RAIL & TRAVEL GATING
- Left rail lists the eight districts by name: Harlem, Detroit, Chicago, Miami, Baltimore, Atlanta, Oakland, NOLA.
- Fast travel is gated by Heat levels (`app.locationHeat`). If a city has active heat (> 0), its button in the rail is disabled, styled red, and labelled with the Heat level (e.g. "DETROIT · HEAT 3").
- Travelling to a clean city updates `app.game.activeCity`, resets weather to Clear, and starts the district BGM and theme.

WALKABILITY & COLLISION RULES
- Double-axis movement handles horizontal and vertical inputs independently.
- Per-axis collision checks player box (16x10 at player's feet) against solid parcels (buildings). This allows the player to slide smoothly along walls instead of sticking.
- Ground, park grass, underpass, and roads are fully walkable.
- Parcels defined as solid cannot be walked into.

CANONICAL POINTS OF INTEREST (POIs)
Each of the eight districts has exactly the five canonical POIs placed at walkable world coordinates:
1. BARBER_SHOP
2. BODEGA
3. SHOP_DEAL
4. CHESS_PARK
5. LOCKED_DOOR
- Names are drawn on the map canvas at the POI coordinates.
- Proximity check: approaching within 52 pixels of a POI updates the screen's prompt line to "[POI NAME] — press ENTER". Pressing Enter triggers the hotspot (enters Ray, Mr. Chen, or T-Bone's NPC interaction scene).

ASSET PACKS & PROCEDURAL FALLBACK
- Asset Registry parses `assets/manifest.json`.
- Sprites use category vocabulary: ground, road, building, furniture, flora, decal, prop, icon.
- Tileset layout specifies 16x16 native tiles with 1-pixel gutters. Mid-rise/high-rise buildings use 32x32.
- Procedural Fallback: if manifest loading, preloading, or an image file fails, the renderer automatically draws vector outlines and filled blocks using the district palette. A missing asset is never fatal.
- Per-District Palettes: Restricted to the 64-colour master gamut (`concrete_kings_64.json`). Large fills (ground, asphalt, building roofs) stay under 120 relative luminance for noir-discipline. Green vegetation uses the cool_tones teal ramp (#0D2926 / #174540 / #246961).

REMOVED AND WHY
- Quest Filter: Removed. The strategic map filter had no data model behind it (quest glyphs were hardcoded at grid cells 4,3 / 16,3 / 16,8 and did not map to actual active quests), so it was purely decorative.
- Fog of War: Removed. There was no explored/visited coordinate tracking in memory, making the visual fog overlay superficial.
- Zoom Controls: Removed. Visual zooming is obsolete under the camera-follow top-down camera system.
- ASCII Map Grid: Removed. Replaced by the 2.5D walkable top-down graphical canvas.
