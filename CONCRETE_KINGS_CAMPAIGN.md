CONCRETE KINGS — SINGLE-PLAYER CAMPAIGN
Noir Street RPG / Origin-Bound Branching Narrative
================================================

NATIVE RESOLUTION: 1280x720
CORE LOOP: EXPLORE -> INTERACT -> PLAY CARD/MINI GAME -> RESOLVE -> REPEAT
MECHANICS INTEGRATION: Trust, Heat, Reputation, Cash, Secrets, Flags, Items
STORY STYLE: Modern noir, grounded, urban, consequence-driven, dialogue-heavy
PLAYER CHARACTER: Custom origin with secret, attributes, appearance

RESEARCH BASIS:
- Three-act structure adapted to branching RPGs: Setup, Confrontation, Resolution
- Branching narrative design: meaningful choices with delayed consequences
- Noir detective/adventure pacing: temperature rise, personal stakes, crisis/climax/conclusion
- Quest design theory: main/side integration, rising urgency, reward pacing
- Reputation/heat systems as narrative drivers, not just stats


================================================
CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW
================================================

TITLE: CONCRETE KINGS — FIRST MILES
SUBTITLE: Every mile costs. Every choice leaves receipts.

STRUCTURE:
  Act 1 — The Setup: 3 days, low heat, introduction to block and core NPCs
  Act 2 — The Confrontation: 7 days, rising heat, central mystery escalates
  Act 3 — The Resolution: 3 days, climax, ending choice, epilogue

TOTAL PLAY TIME: 20-30 minutes for vertical slice/prologue
MAIN QUEST LENGTH: 13 story beats across 3 acts
SIDE QUEST CAPACITY: 12-15 mini quests, many origin-dependent
ENDINGS: 3 canonical endings + 2 secret endings + death states

CAMPAIGN IDENTITY RULES:
- Player origin changes opening scene, NPC attitudes, available shortcuts
- Origin secret is the key to one major branch or late-game reward
- Reputation thresholds unlock district access and NPC loyalty changes
- Heat is both obstacle and resource: high heat enables intimidation, low heat enables stealth
- Cash gates are soft: alternative paths exist for poor characters, but cost time/trust

ACT 1 — THE SETUP (DAYS 1-3)
Theme: Arrival, establishment, first receipts
================================================

ACT 1 GOALS:
- Establish player identity and origin
- Introduce core NPCs: Ray, Jada, Marquez, Mr. Chen, The Kid
- Teach core mechanics through story beats
- Plant mystery seeds: missing kid, corrupt cop, barbershop debt
- Keep heat below 4/10 unless player chooses aggressive path

DAY 1 — ARRIVAL
Beat 1: ORIGIN SCENE
  - Barber: Ray calls you back to the shop after your mom's funeral. Debt is due.
  - Hustler: Marquez picks you up from holding. You owe him a favor.
  - Mechanic: Your ride is seized. Old Man Jenkins offers a cash job.
  - Student: You're expelled. The Dean says leave town or face charges.

  Mechanics: Character creation, origin secret revealed, first trust/heat/cash
  Card Play: None (tutorial)
  Mini Game: None

Beat 2: FIRST BLOCK
  - Player arrives at 125th Street hub
  - Ray introduces barbershop, Jada waves from the bar, Marquez cruises past
  - The Kid asks for help finding his cat
  - Mr. Chen opens bodega with warning: "Heat rises after dark"

  Mechanics: Map tutorial, NPC state colors, basic dialogue
  Card Play: None
  Mini Game: None

Beat 3: THE GOSSIP (FIRST CARD PLAY)
  - Jada at the bar needs a rumor verified
  - Card: "Who's been skimming from the till?"
  - Options: A) Confront Ray directly, B) Ask around quietly, C) Lie to Jada for cash
  - Consequences: trust+/-, heat+/-, secret trigger possible

  Mechanics: First card choice, trust/heat tracking, secret flags
  Card Play: 1 black card, 3-4 white options
  Mini Game: None

DAY 2 — ESTABLISHMENT
Beat 4: WORKING STIFF
  - Ray offers a haircut challenge to prove you still have skills
  - Mini Game: Haircut Challenge (timing windows)
  - Reward: cash, rep + neat, trust with Ray
  - Failure: cash penalty, rep - messy

Beat 5: THE MISSING KID
  - The Kid's brother hasn't come home
  - Quest: Find him at the bodega, alley, or bar
  - Branch: Found sober / Found high / Already gone
  - Card: "How do you handle The Kid's fear?"
  - Consequences: trust with Kid, secret flag, heat if violent

Beat 6: MARQUEZ CHECK-IN
  - Marquez wants a favor: look the other way during a pickup
  - Card: "Marquez offers $50 to ignore the cruiser"
  - Options: A) Take cash and look away, B) Warn the target, C) Refuse openly
  - Consequences: heat+, trust with Marquez, secret flag if warning given

DAY 3 — FIRST HEAT
Beat 7: NIGHT FALLS
  - Heat automatically rises after dark unless player stays at safe house
  - First heat threshold event: street encounter or police sweep
  - If heat >= 4: Marquez confronts player, demands explanation
  - If heat < 4: Jada invites player to back room for private conversation

  Mechanics: Heat as narrative trigger, time-of-day events, safe house value
  Card Play: Branch based on heat level
  Mini Game: None

Beat 8: THE RECEIPT
  - Player receives first "receipt" item: photograph, torn note, or key
  - Receipt ties to origin secret or missing kid subplot
  - Quest log updated: "Follow the receipt"

Beat 9: ACT 1 CLIMAX — THE RAID
  - Police raid the barbershop or bodega
  - Player must choose: hide, run, or talk
  - Card: "Marquez is in the raid. What do you do?"
  - Options depend on heat, trust, origin
    - Barber origin: Ray takes the fall if player has high trust
    - Hustler origin: Marquez helps player escape if owed favor
    - Mechanic origin: Player can hotwire escape car
    - Student origin: Player can use knowledge of back exits

  Consequences: major trust shifts, heat spike, quest flags

ACT 1 ENDING:
- Player escapes or gets arrested
- If arrested: Act 2 starts in holding cell, lower heat, lost cash
- If escaped: Act 2 starts with heat +2, one NPC loyal, one NPC suspicious
- Summary: "Day 3 receipt: you owe something to someone now"

ACT 2 — THE CONFRONTATION (DAYS 4-10)
Theme: Escalation, layered reveals, delayed consequences
================================================

ACT 2 GOALS:
- Central mystery deepens: who is running the pipeline through 125th?
- Origin secret becomes relevant to main plot
- Side quests unlock based on reputation thresholds
- Heat becomes a meaningful obstacle and tool
- Player builds alliances or enemies with lasting impact

DAY 4-5: AFTERMATH
Beat 10: CLEANUP
  - Player must reduce heat or face consequences
  - Options: lay low at safe house, bribe Marquez, do Jada's errand
  - Card: "The block is watching. What's your move?"
  - Mini Game: Negotiation with bodega owner for safe house favor

Beat 11: OLD MAN JENKINS
  - Jenkins reveals he knew player's origin secret all along
  - Offers deal: help him find his niece, he helps player with main mystery
  - Quest: Interview 3 NPCs, each with 1 mini game or card challenge
  - Reward: secret flag, trust with Jenkins, map expansion

DAY 6-7: THE PIPELINE
Beat 12: MR. CHEN'S PROBLEM
  - Chen's shipments are being intercepted
  - Player discovers it's not random—it's organized
  - Card: "Chen offers you a cut to investigate"
  - Branch: Work for Chen, work against Chen, play both sides

Beat 13: THE GOSSIP NETWORK
  - Mini Game: Gossip Network at the bar
  - Connect 5 nodes to reveal who controls the block
  - Success: full suspect list, secret flag
  - Failure: misinformation, heat +1, wrong suspect identified

DAY 8-9: CONFRONTATION
Beat 14: MARQUEZ OR RAY?
  - Depending on prior choices, one NPC becomes the primary contact
  - If trust with Ray > 3: Ray reveals the barbershop is a front
  - If trust with Marquez > 3: Marquez reveals the precinct is compromised
  - Card: "Choose your side for the investigation"
  - Consequences: locks out certain paths, opens others

Beat 15: THE SAFE HOUSE RAID
  - Player's safe house is hit
  - Receipts are stolen or scattered
  - Player must track down who took them
  - Mini Game: Lockpicking or Alley Chase depending on approach

DAY 10: ACT 2 CLIMAX — THE BETRAYAL
Beat 16: THE BETRAYAL
  - One trusted NPC betrays player based on earlier choice
  - Reveal: the pipeline is run by someone player knows
  - Betrayer depends on:
    - High trust with Marquez + low trust with Ray = Marquez betrays
    - High trust with Ray + low trust with Jada = Jada betrays
    - Balanced trusts = Jenkins betrays as wild card
  - Card: "They have The Kid. Negotiate or attack."
  - Options: Pay ransom, storm location, use secret leverage

  Consequences: major heat spike, trust collapse, quest flag

ACT 2 ENDING:
- Player rescues or loses The Kid
- If Kid rescued: Act 3 starts with loyal ally
- If Kid lost: Act 3 starts with guilt, heat +3, secret flag "survivor"
- Summary: "Day 10 receipt: trust is expensive"


================================================
ACT 3 — THE RESOLUTION (DAYS 11-13)
Theme: Final reckoning, origin secret payoff, ending choice
================================================

ACT 3 GOALS:
- Resolve central mystery with player agency
- Origin secret becomes the key to final choice
- All prior branches converge into 3 canonical endings + secrets
- No new major NPCs; all attention on established relationships
- Ending must reflect cumulative trust, heat, reputation, cash

DAY 11: THE CALM BEFORE
Beat 17: PREPARATION
  - Player can do one final side activity before climax
  - Options:
    - Visit Ray: get free haircut, +rep, +1 card modifier
    - Visit Jada: get inside info on target, -heat
    - Visit Chen: get weapons/items, +cash cost
    - Visit Jenkins: get secret leverage on betrayer
  - Mini Game: Optional final challenge for bonus item

Beat 18: THE PLAN
  - Card: "How do you take down the pipeline?"
  - Options depend on accumulated secrets, trusts, heat
    - Legal route: use evidence, involve press, low heat path
    - Street route: rally block, use King of the Block mini game
    - Violent route: storm location, high heat required
    - Secret route: use origin secret for blackmail or leverage
  - Locked options: require specific flags from Act 1-2

DAY 12: THE CLIMAX
Beat 19: THE CONFRONTATION
  - Location depends on chosen route:
    - Legal: courthouse/precinct
    - Street: barbershop/bar
    - Violent: warehouse/dock
    - Secret: safe house/private meeting
  - Card sequence: 3 cards with escalating stakes
    - Card 1: Approach and entry
    - Card 2: The reveal and accusation
    - Card 3: The final choice: justice, mercy, or power
  - Mini Game: Final challenge based on route
    - Legal: Negotiation
    - Street: King of the Block
    - Violent: Alley Chase variant
    - Secret: Burner Phone Hack final

DAY 13: THE RECKONING
Beat 20: THE ENDING
  - Three canonical endings based on final choice AND accumulated stats:

  ENDING A — JUSTICE
    Condition: legal route, evidence collected, betrayer exposed
    Result: corrupt NPC arrested, block reforms, player becomes community leader
    Requirements: trust >= 6 with at least 2 NPCs, heat <= 5, secret flag "evidence"
    Rewards: reputation + community, cash + grant, secret cleared

  ENDING B — POWER
    Condition: street or violent route, high heat, high reputation
    Result: player takes over pipeline, becomes new block authority
    Requirements: rep >= feared, heat >= 8, trust with one NPC = 5
    Rewards: cash flow, district control, flags: "king_of_block"
    Consequences: future playthrough locked to harder mode, new enemies

  ENDING C — GHOST
    Condition: secret route, origin secret used, low heat, low reputation
    Result: player disappears with cash, leaves no receipts, no one knows true outcome
    Requirements: secret flag "origin_secret_used", heat <= 3, rep <= 2
    Rewards: cash + escape fund, secret "new_identity", all flags reset
    Consequences: post-game epilogue shows NPCs wondering what happened

  SECRET ENDING D — THE RECEIPT KING
    Condition: collected all 13 receipts, all secrets, balanced trusts
    Result: player uncovers the true origin of the block's curse and breaks it
    Requirements: flags: all_receipts, all_secrets, trust_balanced
    Rewards: ultimate secret, hidden NPC contact, meta flag for sequel

  SECRET ENDING E — DEATH
    Condition: heat = 10 at any point in Act 3
    Result: player is killed by Marquez or police depending on path
    Consequences: game over, summary of choices, restart option

Beat 21: EPILOGUE
  - 4-6 epilogue cards showing NPC fates based on player choices
  - Each NPC outcome depends on final trust/reputation/heat
  - Final stats screen with all secrets, flags, receipts collected
  - "New Game+" option keeps unlocked secrets, resets story

ORIGIN-INTEGRATED STORY BEATS
================================================

ORIGIN MODIFIERS:
  Each origin changes how beats read, available shortcuts, and late-game options.

BARBER ORIGIN:
  - Opening: Ray calls you back, shop debt is personal
  - Shortcut: free haircuts = +1 charisma modifier in bar scenes
  - Mid-game: Ray offers inside info if you fix his mistake
  - Late-game: can frame evidence using barbershop records
  - Secret: "shop debt" -> unlocks hidden ledger with incriminating dates

HUSTLER ORIGIN:
  - Opening: Marquez picks you up, you owe a favor
  - Shortcut: street dice mini games have +2 WIT bonus
  - Mid-game: Marquez offers inside police info if you run a package
  - Late-game: can bribe or bluff your way through precinct
  - Secret: "marked by Marquez" -> blackmail leverage or death mark

MECHANIC ORIGIN:
  - Opening: Jenkins offers cash job, your car is seized
  - Shortcut: lockpicking mini games have +2 STR bonus
  - Mid-game: can hotwire any vehicle, disable security systems
  - Late-game: can rig warehouse/bar escape route
  - Secret: "stolen car" -> hidden compartment with evidence

STUDENT ORIGIN:
  - Opening: Dean expulsion, charges pending
  - Shortcut: burner phone hack has +2 WIT bonus
  - Mid-game: library records reveal property ownership history
  - Late-game: can leak evidence to press or use legal loophole
  - Secret: "expelled for fighting" -> school records prove self-defense

ORIGIN CROSSOVER:
  - By Day 5, all origin paths converge at Jenkins' safe house
  - Jenkins reveals he knows each secret and offers trade
  - Player must choose: keep secret hidden or trade it for main quest progress
  - This choice determines one Act 3 branch availability

SIDE QUEST SYSTEM
================================================

SIDE QUEST DESIGN RULES:
  - Each side quest ties to main plot through NPC, location, or receipt
  - Side quest completion modifies main quest options in Act 3
  - Side quests are origin-neutral unless specified
  - Max 2 side quests per day to prevent pacing drag

SIDE QUEST CATALOG:

SQ1: THE CAT (Day 1)
  Trigger: The Kid
  Objective: Find cat in alley or bodega
  Reward: trust + Kid, secret flag "cat_found"
  Integration: Kid provides intel in Act 2 if completed

SQ2: RAY'S DEBT (Day 2)
  Trigger: Ray
  Objective: Collect $100 from former client
  Mini Game: Negotiation or Street Dice
  Reward: cash, trust + Ray, reduces shop debt
  Integration: Ray's loyalty determines Act 1 climax outcome

SQ3: CHEN'S SHIPMENT (Day 4)
  Trigger: Mr. Chen
  Objective: Guard or intercept shipment
  Branch: honest / corrupt / double-cross
  Reward: cash, trust + Chen, secret flag "shipment_route"
  Integration: Reveals pipeline structure for Act 2

SQ4: JADA'S SECRET (Day 5)
  Trigger: Jada, trust >= 3
  Objective: Retrieve her journal from ex-husband
  Mini Game: Alley Chase or Negotiation
  Reward: trust + Jada, secret flag "jada_past"
  Integration: Jada's loyalty determines Act 2 betrayal

SQ5: THE OLD MAN'S STORY (Day 6)
  Trigger: Jenkins, trust >= 2
  Objective: Find Jenkins' old service medal
  Location: pawn shop or precinct
  Reward: secret flag "jenkins_mercy", rep + elders
  Integration: Jenkins' allegiance determined here

SQ6: MARQUEZ'S FAVOR (Day 7)
  Trigger: Marquez, trust >= 3
  Objective: Plant evidence or intimidate witness
  Mini Game: Lockpicking or Negotiation
  Reward: heat + Marquez trust, secret flag "dirty_cop"
  Integration: Marquez betrayer/ally path locked here

SQ7: THE BODEGA QUIZ (Day 2)
  Trigger: Mr. Chen, casual
  Objective: Answer 5 neighborhood trivia questions
  Mini Game: Gossip Network variant
  Reward: cash, rep + neighborhood, secret flag "local_history"
  Integration: Trivia answers become clues in Act 2

SQ8: BARBER CONTEST (Day 3)
  Trigger: Ray, shop hours
  Objective: Win haircut speed contest
  Mini Game: Barber Challenge rhythm
  Reward: cash pot, rep + neat, trust + Ray
  Integration: Ray's pride affects Act 1 raid choice

SQ9: THE PHONE HACK (Day 4)
  Trigger: Tech NPC or safe house
  Objective: Hack burner phone for caller ID
  Mini Game: Burner Phone Hack
  Reward: secret flag "caller_id", trust + tech contact
  Integration: Reveals betrayer identity early if successful

SQ10: KING OF THE BLOCK (Day 5)
  Trigger: Block event at park
  Objective: Win 3-round voting contest
  Mini Game: King of the Block social
  Reward: rep + district control, flag "block_leader"
  Integration: Unlocks street route in Act 3

SQ11: CRAFTING THE RATCHET (Day 6)
  Trigger: Mechanic NPC
  Objective: Gather 3 parts, craft lockpick set
  Mini Game: Crafting resource selection
  Reward: item: lockpick_set (+2 to all lock mini games)
  Integration: Enables extra path in Act 2 safe house raid

SQ12: THE NEGOTIATION (Day 7)
  Trigger: Debt collector at bar
  Objective: Settle debt without paying full amount
  Mini Game: Negotiation bid/ask
  Reward: cash saved, trust + bar patrons, rep + feared
  Integration: Cash reserve affects Act 3 preparation options

SIDE QUEST PLACEMENT RULES:
  - Day 1-3: SQ1, SQ2, SQ7, SQ8
  - Day 4-7: SQ3, SQ4, SQ5, SQ6, SQ9, SQ10, SQ11, SQ12
  - Day 8-10: No new side quests; only main plot beats
  - If player completes SQ10: unlocks additional Act 3 street options
  - If player completes SQ9: reveals betrayer identity in Act 2


================================================
QUEST PACING & HEAT/REP INTEGRATION
================================================

PACE CURVE:
  Day 1-2: 1 main beat + 0-1 side quests, heat 0-2
  Day 3: 1 main beat + climax, heat spike to 4-6
  Day 4-5: 1 main beat + 1-2 side quests, heat 3-5
  Day 6-7: 1 main beat + 1-2 side quests, heat 4-7
  Day 8-9: 1 main beat + climax, heat 6-9
  Day 10: 1 main beat + ending choice, heat 7-10 or reset

HEAT AS NARRATIVE:
  - Heat 0-3: Low profile, stealth paths available, NPCs friendly
  - Heat 4-6: Notice by police, Marquez watching, intimidation options open
  - Heat 7-9: Active warrants, safe house compromised, violence likely
  - Heat 10: Death ending possible, police raid every location

REPUTATION AS NARRATIVE:
  - Rep 0-2: Ghost, no one knows you, easy to disappear
  - Rep 3-5: Known quantity, NPCs reference your actions
  - Rep 6-8: Feared/Respected, doors open, prices drop, access granted
  - Rep 9-10: Block legend, ending locked to Power or Justice path

TRUST AS NARRATIVE:
  - Trust is per-NPC, 0-5 scale
  - Trust 0: NPC hostile or unavailable
  - Trust 1-2: Neutral, basic interactions only
  - Trust 3-4: Loyal, will help in Act 2-3 climaxes
  - Trust 5: Maximum loyalty, origin secret shared, unique ending options

REACTIVE CONTENT LAYER
================================================

This campaign supports deepening systems defined in companion files. These
systems sit on top of the core 13-beat structure and do not replace it.

REFERENCE FILES:
- C:\Users\thecr\concrete-kings\storylets.json — reactive 1-2 card encounters injected between main beats
- C:\Users\thecr\concrete-kings\origin_relationships.md — per-origin NPC trust tables, shortcuts, and Act 3 roles
- C:\Users\thecr\concrete-kings\CONCRETE_KINGS_CONTENT_DEEPENING.md — full deepening system reference

REACTIVE NPC RELATIONSHIP WEB:
- NPCs remember player actions and reference them in dialogue
- Relationship states: allied, neutral, rival, hostile
- Joint scenes evaluate relationship states at scene start
- Betrayer is selected at Day 8 based on trust delta, not raw values
- Player can avoid betrayal by raising lowest-trust NPC before Day 8

STORYLET SYSTEM:
- 7 starter storylets in storylets.json; expandable to 50+
- Storylets trigger by location, heat, flags, act, time, and probability
- Cooldowns prevent repetition; once-per-playthrough storylets use cooldownDays: 999
- Storylet choices must have visible effect within 2-3 beats
- No storylet can override main quest climax

ENVIRONMENTAL STORYTELLING:
- Graffiti changes across acts to reflect world control
- Receipts and abandoned objects provide lore fragments when inspected
- NPC schedules shift based on trust and world state
- Audio cues change meaning based on heat and reputation

DELAYED CONSEQUENCE CHAINS:
- Every major choice writes to consequenceChains with trigger and effect
- Effects resolve at day/act transitions, not immediately
- Unresolved chains auto-resolve at climax if conditions never met

WORLD STATE CHANGES:
- Locations transform based on player actions
- World state affects NPC schedules, storylet availability, and ending paths
- All state changes are permanent within the playthrough

NEW GAME+ META:
- Lore fragments and secrets carry forward
- NPCs reference previous playthrough if origin-specific endings were reached
- Secret endings unlock additional dialogue in early acts

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES:
- All deepening systems are data-driven
- storylets.json is the source of truth for reactive encounters
- origin_relationships.md is the source of truth for NPC trust tables
- Systems must be disableable for performance and accessibility
